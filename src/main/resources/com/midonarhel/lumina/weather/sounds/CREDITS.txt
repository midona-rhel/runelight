Weather sounds. All recordings are CC0 1.0 (public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They were made into the files here by tools/make_weather_sounds.py (mono, 22.05 kHz, 16 bit). The player muffles the
rain and the thunder indoors as it mixes (AudioMixer).

rain.wav, rain_light.wav
  "Rain 1" by DledeSOUND, https://freesound.org/people/DledeSOUND/sounds/875651/ (CC0, licence read from the Freesound
  page; the 185 kbps preview MP3 was used). The light loop is the source pitched down and cut at 3.5 kHz, layered; the
  heavy one is a spectral wash of the source layered many times and compressed.

thunder1.wav  "Thunder Clap2" by roboroo, https://freesound.org/people/roboroo/sounds/436794/ (CC0-1.0)
thunder2.wav  "Thunder 1 (No Reverb)" by Terry93D, https://freesound.org/people/Terry93D/sounds/327986/ (CC0-1.0)
thunder3.wav  the same recording as thunder2.wav, played at 0.7 speed (slower and lower)
  Taken from kocheck/Graphium, macos/App/Graphium/Sounds, whose ledger.json records each one's creator, source and
  licence CC0-1.0 (checked 2026-10-18). Each is faded in over 0.3 s by tools/make_weather_sounds.py.
