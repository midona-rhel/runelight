Weather sounds. All four recordings are CC0 1.0 (public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They were made into the files here by tools/make_weather_sounds.py (mono, 22.05 kHz, 16 bit; the rain looped, the
thunder trimmed and faded). The player muffles the rain and the thunder indoors as it mixes (AudioMixer).

  Resources/AudioCredits.txt names the creator, the source and the licence CC0 (checked 2026-10-25). The loop is the
  steady 10 s cut from 174.5 s of it.
